About Rupa Classes

Rupa Classes is a small, neighbourhood nursery class based in Sai Nagar, Amravati. We focus on early learners — building the basics of language, numbers and everyday skills through play, rhymes, stories and simple activities.

Classes are kept small so every child gets attention, and parents are always welcome to ask questions about how their child is doing.

  • Small batch sizes for individual attention
  • Focus on nursery-level foundations
  • Friendly, familiar, local setting
  • Regular updates to parents

What We Cover

A simple, steady routine designed for young children.

Letters & Sounds

Introducing the alphabet, phonics and early reading habits through songs and picture cards.

Numbers & Counting

Counting, shapes and simple number recognition using hands-on activities.

Rhymes & Stories

Poems, stories and group activities that build listening and speaking skills.

Good Habits

Sharing, listening, following simple instructions and everyday manners.
